The Format

A Four-Week Championship Battle

Each night runs as a complete race weekend: a driver briefing, practice, qualifying, and two 20-minute races — with Race 2 run on a reverse grid to mix things up. Race across four circuits — Vallelunga Classic, Nürburgring Sprint, Laguna Seca, and Zandvoort — earning points every week toward the overall championship.
